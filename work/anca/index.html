Visual identity, Art Direction, Design

Algerian Cinema Digital Archives, 2026. Visual identity, art direction, graphic design

Development of a new visual identity for the Algerian Cinema Digital Archives (ANCA), an initiative dedicated to preserving and promoting Algeria’s cinematic heritage.

The project encompasses the visual identity and its graphic applications, establishing a coherent visual language for the archive.

In collaboration with Nabil Djedouani, founder of the archives.
